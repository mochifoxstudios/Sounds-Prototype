Story phases

One theme per chapter, heavier as the story goes on. The same five-note Operator motif turns up in every one; listen for it.

ACQUIRE click

Four variants per phase on one sound, each tuned to a note of that phase's key. They play at random and never repeat back to back, so fast clicking plays a melody.
